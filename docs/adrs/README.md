# Architectural Decision Records (ADRs)

This directory records architectural and technical decisions made during the design and evolution of RepoPilot.

## ADR Structure

Each ADR captures:
- **Context**: The background, driving forces, and constraints motivating the decision.
- **Decision**: The chosen technical approach, architecture, or pattern.
- **Alternatives Considered**: Other viable options evaluated and why they were rejected.
- **Consequences**: The trade-offs, positive benefits, and operational/maintenance costs of the decision.
